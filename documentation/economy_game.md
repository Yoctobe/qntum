PLAYER CONTROLS (The Levers)
│
├── MONETARY POLICY (Fed Tools)
│   ├── Federal Funds Rate        (0% - 20%)
│   ├── QE / QT                   (buy or sell bonds, amount per month)
│   ├── Reserve Requirement       (% banks must hold)
│   ├── Discount Window Rate      (rate Fed lends to banks directly)
│   └── Forward Guidance          (words - dovish/hawkish signal)
│
├── FISCAL POLICY (Gov Tools)
│   ├── Government Spending       (increase/decrease %)
│   ├── Tax Rate                  (corporate + income)
│   ├── Bond Issuance             (how much debt to issue)
│   └── Tariffs                   (on imports %)
│
└── EMERGENCY TOOLS
    ├── Bank Bailout              (inject capital to failing banks)
    ├── Currency Intervention     (buy/sell own currency)
    └── Debt Ceiling Override     (political crisis trigger)

ECONOMIC INDICATORS (The Dashboard)
│
├── INFLATION
│   ├── CPI                       (target 2%)
│   ├── PCE                       (Fed preferred)
│   ├── PPI (Producer Price)      (leading indicator, comes before CPI)
│   └── Inflation Expectations    (survey based, self fulfilling)
│
├── EMPLOYMENT
│   ├── Unemployment Rate         (target ~4%)
│   ├── U6 Underemployment        (hidden unemployment)
│   ├── NFP Monthly Jobs Added    (target +150-200k healthy)
│   └── Wage Growth %             (too high = inflation risk)
│
├── GROWTH
│   ├── GDP Growth Rate           (target ~2%)
│   ├── Consumer Spending         (70% of US GDP)
│   ├── Business Investment       (capex)
│   └── Housing Market            (rate sensitive, early signal)
│
├── DEBT & FISCAL
│   ├── National Debt / GDP ratio (danger zone >130%)
│   ├── Annual Deficit            (spending - revenue)
│   ├── Interest Payment / GDP    (critical threshold)
│   └── Credit Rating             (AAA to D, affects yield)
│
├── MARKETS
│   ├── 10Y Bond Yield            (trust thermometer)
│   ├── Yield Curve               (normal / inverted = recession signal)
│   ├── Stock Market              (wealth effect on spending)
│   └── Dollar Index DXY          (currency strength vs basket)
│
└── EXTERNAL
    ├── Trade Balance             (exports - imports)
    ├── Current Account           (broader trade picture)
    ├── Foreign Reserves          (buffer against crisis)
    └── Capital Flows             (money coming in or leaving)

SCENARIOS TO BUILD (Game Modes)
│
├── 2008 Financial Crisis         bank collapse, liquidity freeze
├── 1970s Stagflation             high inflation + high unemployment
│                                 hardest scenario, no good move
├── COVID Stimulus Hangover       printed too much, now fight inflation
├── Emerging Market Crisis        currency collapse, foreign debt trap
├── Bond Market Revolt            yields spike, lose market confidence
├── Debt Spiral                   deficit grows faster than GDP
└── Soft Landing Challenge        cool inflation without recession
    (what Powell attempted 2022-2024)

─────────────────────────────────────────────

CAUSE & EFFECT ENGINE (Game Logic)
│
├── Rate Hike +0.25%
│   → mortgage rates rise in 1 quarter
│   → housing slows in 2 quarters
│   → consumer spending drops in 3 quarters
│   → unemployment rises in 4 quarters
│   → CPI drops in 4-6 quarters
│   → bond yields react immediately
│
├── QE (print/buy bonds)
│   → liquidity injected immediately
│   → stock market rises fast
│   → inflation rises in 2-4 quarters
│   → dollar weakens
│   → imports become expensive
│
├── Tax Cut
│   → consumer spending up immediately
│   → deficit widens
│   → bond issuance increases
│   → yields may rise
│   → growth short term, debt long term
│
└── Tariff Increase
    → import prices rise immediately
    → CPI spike
    → trading partners retaliate
    → exports fall
    → growth slows

─────────────────────────────────────────────

GAME HEALTH METERS
│
├── Public Trust          (tank this = bond revolt)
├── Market Confidence     (tank this = stock crash)
├── Bank Stability        (tank this = credit freeze)
├── Political Pressure    (congress vs fed independence)
└── Global Confidence     (tank this = dollar loses reserve status)
    (ultimate game over)

─────────────────────────────────────────────

LAG TIMER (Most Realistic Feature)
Every action you take has a delayed effect
Player must act on OLD data while NEW consequences
are already baking in the background
This is what makes Fed policy genuinely hard
